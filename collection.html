Every time you use an app, visit a website or create an online account, information about you may be collected. Understanding how data collection works helps you make informed choices about what you share.

01 // DATA COLLECTION

HOW IS DATA COLLECTED?

Data collection is the process of gathering information about users through websites, apps and online services.

COMMON METHODS

  • Online forms and registrations
  • Cookies and website activity
  • Mobile applications
  • Online purchases
  • Surveys and feedback
02 // DATA COLLECTION

WHY IS DATA COLLECTED?

Websites and apps collect data to provide their services, improve user experience and understand how people use their products.

Some information is necessary for a service to work, while other data may be collected for personalization, analytics or advertising.

BEFORE YOU SHARE

  • Check what information is being requested
  • Read important privacy settings
  • Share only what is necessary
  • Review permissions when needed
