Artificial intelligence can process large amounts of information to generate responses, recommendations and other outputs. Understanding what data is involved is important when using AI-powered services.

01 // AI & DATA

HOW DOES AI USE DATA?

AI systems can process information provided by users, depending on the service and how it is designed. This information may be used to generate or improve outputs.

The type of information involved can vary between AI services. Personal information should therefore be handled carefully when interacting with AI tools.

THINK BEFORE SHARING

  • Personal details
  • Private conversations
  • Photos and documents
  • Passwords or sensitive information
02 // AI PRIVACY

USE AI RESPONSIBLY

AI can be useful for learning, creating and solving problems, but users should understand what information they provide to an AI service.

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information unless you understand why it is needed and how the service handles that information.

PRIVACY AWARENESS

  • Check what information you share
  • Avoid sensitive personal details
  • Review available privacy settings
  • Think before uploading private content
