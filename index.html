01 // PERSONAL DATA

PERSONAL DATA

Personal data is any information that can identify a person directly or indirectly. In the digital world, we share this information with websites, apps, social media platforms and online services.

Your name, email address, phone number, location, photographs, passwords and online activity can all be connected to you. Even information that seems harmless can become sensitive when combined with other data.

COMMON EXAMPLES

  • Name and contact information
  • Location and device information
  • Photos and social media activity
  • Online searches and browsing history
  • Account and login information
02 // DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

A digital footprint is the trail of information you leave behind while using the internet. Every search, website visit, social media interaction and online activity can contribute to this footprint.

Some activities are shared intentionally, while others may be recorded automatically by websites, apps and online services. Over time, these activities can create a detailed picture of your online behavior.

WHERE IT COMES FROM

  • Social media posts and interactions
  • Website visits and online searches
  • Mobile apps and device activity
  • Online shopping and purchases
  • Location and browsing activity
03 // PRIVACY RISKS

PRIVACY RISKS

Privacy risks can occur when personal information is exposed, misused, accessed without permission or shared with the wrong people. Small mistakes in online activities can sometimes create serious privacy problems.

Phishing messages, weak passwords, excessive app permissions and fake websites are some common ways in which personal data can be put at risk. Being aware of these risks can help you make safer decisions online.

COMMON RISKS

  • Phishing and suspicious links
  • Weak or reused passwords
  • Unnecessary app permissions
  • Fake websites and online scams
  • Oversharing personal information
  • Social engineering attacks
04 // DATA PROTECTION

DATA PROTECTION

Data protection means taking steps to keep your personal information safe from unauthorized access, misuse and unnecessary exposure.

Good privacy habits can reduce the chances of your information being compromised. Simple actions such as using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication and checking app permissions can make a difference.

PROTECT YOUR DATA

  • Use strong and unique passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Review app permissions regularly
  • Avoid suspicious links and websites
  • Keep apps and devices updated
  • Think before sharing sensitive information
