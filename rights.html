Privacy rights give people more control over how their personal information is collected, used and shared. Understanding these rights can help you make informed choices about your data.

01 // PRIVACY RIGHTS

WHAT ARE PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Privacy rights are rules and protections that can give individuals control over their personal information and how organizations handle it.

Depending on the applicable law, people may have rights related to accessing, correcting, deleting or controlling the use of their personal data.

COMMON RIGHTS

  • Right to access information
  • Right to correct certain data
  • Right to request deletion in some cases
  • Right to understand data usage
02 // USER AWARENESS

TAKE CONTROL

Knowing your privacy rights helps you understand what information you are providing and how it may be handled.

Before sharing personal information, check the privacy policy, permissions and available controls provided by the service.

STAY INFORMED

  • Read important privacy information
  • Check available privacy controls
  • Understand what you are agreeing to
  • Ask questions when something is unclear
