TRACKING
YOU?

Online services can track different aspects of your activity to understand how you interact with websites and applications. This tracking can happen through cookies, device information and other technologies.

01 // ONLINE TRACKING

HOW DOES TRACKING WORK?

Tracking technologies can record information such as the websites you visit, your interactions and certain details about the device you use.

Cookies, pixels and device identifiers are commonly used to recognize activity across online services and build patterns of user behavior.

COMMON TRACKING METHODS

  • Cookies
  • Tracking pixels
  • Device identifiers
  • Location tracking
02 // TRACKING AWARENESS

WHAT CAN YOU DO?

Understanding tracking helps you make better decisions about the websites, apps and services you use.

Reviewing privacy settings, managing permissions and checking cookie choices can help you have more control over your online activity.

STAY AWARE

  • Review cookie settings
  • Check app permissions
  • Use privacy controls
  • Be aware of what you share
